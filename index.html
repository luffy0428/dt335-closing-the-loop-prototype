INT335 CA2 Project · Closing the Loop

Problem P02: "Hostel Maintenance Complaints That Go Nowhere". Below is the Design Thinking synthesis, empathy research, personas, journey mapping, and idea screening matrix that drove the LoopFix architecture.

AS

Aryan Sharma

2nd Year B.Tech Hosteller · Block-B

"I filed a ticket 4 days ago about my water geyser. I had no idea if anyone read it, so I had to personally go to the warden's office during study hours."

Key Pain Points:

  • Zero feedback loop or ETA after filing tickets.
  • Technicians closing tickets without visiting ("Ghost closures").
  • Must stay locked in room waiting uncertainly.
  • Forced into duplicate complaints out of anxiety.

Design Interventions:

  • Mutual 4-digit OTP closure verification.
  • Live queue position and SLA countdown window.
  • 1-click direct escalation when ticket stalls.
RK

Ramesh Kumar

Senior Electrician · Maintenance Staff

"Students think we sit idle, but our paper job list is scattered across 4 blocks with no inventory tracking. When parts run out, we get blamed."

Key Pain Points:

  • Juggling random paper slips with conflicting priorities.
  • Wasting hours walking back and forth without floor grouping.
  • Accused of delays while waiting for central store spare parts.
  • Students not available in rooms during unannounced visits.

Design Interventions:

  • Floor & block route-clustered mobile job cards.
  • One-tap "Spare Parts Pause" with automatic student notification.
  • Photo-proof upload & OTP sign-off eliminates disputes.
